Privacy Policy: Hermes

Who it belongs to: Hermes is a personal app belonging to Betsalel, and only he uses it.

What it can access:

  • Google Drive: only files the app itself created. It has no access to any other files.
  • Google Calendar: calendar events, in order to create, read and update them.

How the data is used: only for Betsalel's personal assistance. It is not sold, not shared, and not transferred to anyone else.

How to revoke access: remove Hermes at myaccount.google.com/connections. Files that were created remain yours.
